# Product vision

## Purpose

Help a builder turn one defensible, useful idea into a public Agent Skill that is genuinely installable and easy to understand.

## Users

Independent builders and domain experts who want skills to serve as useful open-source products and a durable distribution channel.

## Product promise

Given explicit launch authorization, Skill Create researches the market, selects a narrow gap, builds a complete repository, publishes it, and proves each release layer separately.

## Principles

- Usefulness before attention
- Narrow promises before generic bundles
- Fresh evidence before assumptions
- Original and rights-safe content
- Explicit authorization for external actions
- Verifiable publication, not status theatre

## Out of scope

- Buying stars, manipulating rankings, or manufacturing engagement
- Personalized financial, medical, or legal advice
- Social posting or outreach without separate approval
- Uploading private or proprietary data
