# Idea selection

Score each candidate from 1 to 5. Write one sentence of evidence for every score.

| Factor | Question | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| Repeated pain | Does the problem recur often enough to justify a reusable skill? | 25% |
| Existing demand | Do current searches, repos, issues, or discussions show real interest? | 20% |
| Gap strength | Is the proposed promise meaningfully narrower, safer, or more complete than alternatives? | 20% |
| Builder fit | Does the user have credible knowledge, workflows, or assets for this problem? | 15% |
| Demo clarity | Can a stranger understand and try the value within five minutes? | 10% |
| Maintenance | Can the skill stay useful without fragile services or constant updates? | 10% |

Reject an idea even with a high score when it depends on unlicensed content, hidden credentials, personalized regulated advice, fabricated evidence, or a misleading name.

Prefer a sharp job such as “turn a messy filing into a source-linked diligence checklist” over a broad label such as “investment assistant.”
